Why does this exist?
Send an LLM the same prompt twice and you can get two different answers. That's by design: the model picks each next token by sampling from a probability distribution. Temperature controls how adventurous that sampling is.
Closest thing you already know: a random seed in your tests. The difference is that here you tune how much randomness you get, not just whether it repeats.
Why you care: extraction and classification want low temperature (consistent), brainstorming wants higher (varied).

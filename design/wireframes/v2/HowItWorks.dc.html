Step 4, at your pace

Learn only the gaps

A tutor teaches one topic at a time, in the order the prerequisites need. It remembers what you know and what you got wrong, so it never re-explains the basics. Already know it? Skip straight to the test.

Gap mode, for bridge topics Starts from your actual mistake, connects it to what you know, then shows where the analogy breaks.
Novice mode, for new topics A worked example first, then the same example with blanks, then you write it yourself.
Step 5, a few minutes per topic

Prove it, then move on

Each topic ends with fresh questions you haven't seen. Pass and it turns Known on your map, which unlocks what comes next. Miss one and the tutor picks up exactly there next time.
